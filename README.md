# fishing-holiday-project
HolidayVibe Maldives offers a countrywide sport-fishing holiday and private charter program designed for travel agencies, specialist fishing companies, small groups and individual anglers.
The program combines professionally coordinated fishing experiences with accommodation, meals, domestic transfers and activities for accompanying partners or families.

Fishing holidays can be arranged across Vaavu, Ari, Laamu, Huvadhoo, Malé and Hulhumalé, with additional locations available according to the client’s preferred species, fishing techniques, travel dates and budget. Programs can include popping, spinning, jigging, trolling, live-bait fishing, reef fishing and selected fly-fishing opportunities. Common targets include giant trevally, bluefin trevally, dogtooth tuna, yellowfin tuna, wahoo, mahi-mahi, barracuda, grouper, snapper, emperor, sailfish and marlin.

HolidayVibe provides access to different vessel sizes and charter formats, from practical boats for two to four anglers to larger premium vessels suitable for serious tropical fishing. Half-day, full-day and multi-day programs can be arranged. Fishing equipment, tackle, refreshments, meals and other inclusions vary by program and are clearly stated in each quotation.

Accommodation options range from affordable guesthouses on local islands to comfortable beachfront hotels, luxury resorts and liveaboards. HolidayVibe can arrange bed and breakfast, half-board or full-board packages, together with airport transfers, domestic flights, speedboat connections and inter-island logistics. Resort stays can also be combined with privately arranged fishing days, allowing non-angling guests to enjoy beaches, spas, diving and other activities.

The document presents indicative seven-night programs for small groups, with five or six fishing sessions and clear per-person estimates for different group sizes. It also includes destination positioning, recommended fishing methods, boat specifications, seasonal guidance, accommodation choices and planning conditions.

Every itinerary is prepared as a complete HolidayVibe package. Final rates are confirmed according to exact dates, room category, meal plan, vessel capacity, pickup location and fishing requirements. Weather, tides and fish movement influence each day’s plan, and catches cannot be guaranteed.

HolidayVibe welcomes B2B partnerships and prepares tailored quotations for agencies worldwide. International flights are excluded, while all Maldives land arrangements can be coordinated through one local point of contact. Clients receive local assistance before arrival and throughout the trip, helping agencies deliver smooth, flexible and well-organized Maldives fishing holidays.
